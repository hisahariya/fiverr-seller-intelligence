    Real benchmarks, built by sellers

    Most "Fiverr data" online is guesswork. We're building category benchmarks from real gigs instead, and sellers can opt in to share their numbers anonymously when they run a Health Check.

    • Only the category and numbers are shared (prices, delivery, reviews, rating). Never titles, descriptions or tags.
    • Sharing is off by default. You choose to turn it on every time.
    • As contributions grow, benchmarks like "typical Premium price in Logo Design" come from real data, not estimates.

    Safe for your Fiverr account

    Your account is your income, so the toolkit never touches it.

    No Fiverr login, everWe never ask for your password or access to your account.
    Nothing is scraped from FiverrYou paste your own text in. We don't crawl Fiverr's site, so there's nothing that breaks its rules.
    Your text stays on your deviceTitles, descriptions and tags are checked in your browser. Sharing your numbers is optional and off by default.
    Is it really free?

    Yes. The Gig Builder, Health Check and Category Intelligence are free, with no signup and no daily limit. The Gig Makeover is an optional paid service.

    Can it get my Fiverr account in trouble?

    No. You copy and paste text into our tools. We never log in to Fiverr and never collect data from it.

    Where do the keywords and prices come from?

    From a dataset of 40 categories we put together as guidance. Prices are suggested starting points, not live Fiverr data, so always compare with the top gigs in your category.

    Can it show where my gig ranks?

    Not yet. Rank checking needs Fiverr data we won't scrape. Instead, we're building benchmarks from sellers who choose to share their numbers.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes. Every tool works on mobile, with nothing to install.
